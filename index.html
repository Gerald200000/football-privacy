Football Quiz Legends

Privacy Policy

Developer: PureForm Solutions · Effective date: 10 November 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Football Quiz Legends (the "Game"), made by PureForm Solutions ("we", "us"), handles information when you play it on your Android device.

In short: we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal details. The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress, such as your coin total, your best win and the number of games played, only on your device. This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is supported by ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language;
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them, such as views and taps.

Depending on your choices and where you live, Google may use this information to show personalised ads. Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses advertising

Rewarded ads: some features, such as an extra lifeline, a second chance or doubling your coins, are unlocked by choosing to watch an ad. Watching these ads is always optional.

4. Your consent and choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the Game shows Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time using the Privacy Settings button on the Game's home screen.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings (on most Android devices: Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Stop all data collection: you can stop all collection by the Game at any time by uninstalling it.

5. Game engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. Unity may collect limited technical information, such as device type and performance data, to operate and improve its engine. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help to have it removed.

7. Sharing and selling of information

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and engine services described above, we do not share information from the Game with anyone.

8. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies. To ask questions about your data or request deletion, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

We use the security features provided by Android and by Google's services. However, no method of transmitting or storing data is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
